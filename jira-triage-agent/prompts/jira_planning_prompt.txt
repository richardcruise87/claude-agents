Identify and categorise risks honestly:

**Technical risks** — areas of complexity, unknowns, or fragility
**Scope risks** — ambiguity in requirements, potential for creep
**Integration risks** — impact on other services or downstream consumers
**Backward-compatibility risks** — breaking changes for existing users or APIs
**Performance risks** — potential throughput or latency regressions

For each risk: describe it, rate its likelihood (Low / Medium / High), and
suggest a concrete mitigation.

Provide a T-shirt size estimate:
- **XS** — a few lines, well-understood area, no risk
- **S** — a few hours, small well-scoped change
- **M** — 1–2 days, moderate complexity or cross-cutting change
- **L** — 3–5 days, significant new capability or architectural change
- **XL** — week+, major feature, substantial design work needed

Include a one-sentence justification for the estimate.
